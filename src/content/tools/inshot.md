## Overview

**InShot** is the go-to pocket video and photo editor for millions of content creators, influencers, and digital marketers worldwide. Focused on simplicity, lightning-fast turnaround times, and social-first aesthetics, InShot turns raw smartphone clips into polished Instagram Reels, TikToks, and YouTube Shorts in minutes.

## Core Feature Highlights

- **Instant Canvas Aspect Ratios**: Convert horizontal 16:9 landscape video into 9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, or 4:5 Instagram feed format with blurred background padding.
- **Precision Trimming & Splitting**: Intuitive tap-to-split handles to slice away pauses, bad takes, and filler words with zero learning curve.
- **Audio & Voiceover Tools**: Record built-in microphone voiceovers, extract audio from existing videos, and access hundreds of sound effects (swishes, laughter, bells).
- **Stickers, Keyframes & Text**: Add animated subtitles, kinetic emojis, and keyframe position moves for sticker callouts.
