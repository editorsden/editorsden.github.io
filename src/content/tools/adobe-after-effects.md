## Overview

**Adobe After Effects** is the motion graphics and visual effects foundation of digital video production. While standard NLEs handle horizontal timeline cuts, After Effects is a 2.5D and 3D spatial compositing canvas designed for kinetic typography, HUD interfaces, green screen cleanups, and title sequences.

## Essential Capabilities

- **AI Roto Brush 3.0**: Separate foreground actors and complex hair strands from busy backgrounds without tedious frame-by-frame pen tool masking.
- **Mocha AE Planar Tracking**: Built-in planar tracker from Boris FX for screen replacements, logo corner-pins, and object removals.
- **Dynamic Link**: Seamlessly embed Ae comps into Premiere Pro sequences without intermediate exports.
- **Expression Engine**: JavaScript-based expressions allow procedural motion automation, wiggles, dampeners, and synchronized audio-reactive elements.
