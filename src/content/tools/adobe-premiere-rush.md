## Overview

**Adobe Premiere Rush** is Adobe's streamlined, all-in-one video creation tool designed for creators who shoot, edit, and publish video across multiple devices. Rush bridges the gap between simple mobile video apps and complex desktop NLEs by allowing mobile edits to open directly inside full Premiere Pro.

## Key Capabilities

- **Shoot, Edit, Share**: Built-in professional camera mode on iOS and Android with manual ISO, shutter speed, and white balance control.
- **Cross-Device Cloud Project Sync**: Edits made on an iPad or phone automatically synchronize to your desktop workstation through Creative Cloud storage.
- **Direct Premiere Pro Handoff**: Open any Rush project directly inside Premiere Pro with all cut points, color adjustments, audio tracks, and titles preserved as native sequence tracks.
- **Essential Sound Audio Auto-Ducking**: Automatically dips background music volume whenever human voice dialogue is detected.
