## Overview

**Upscayl** is a powerhouse desktop application that brings state-of-the-art AI upscaling directly to your local GPU without requiring expensive cloud subscriptions or subscription credits.

### Highlights for Editors

- **100% Local & Private**: No files are sent to third-party servers. All inference runs locally on your Mac, Windows, or Linux GPU via Vulkan.
- **Batch Image & Frame Processing**: Upscale entire folders of image sequences, archival photos, or YouTube thumbnail assets at once.
- **Multiple AI Models**: Switch between photo enhancement, digital art, fast general upscaling, and sharpness-focused models depending on the source material.
