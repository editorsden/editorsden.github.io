## Overview

Think of **Blip** as cross-platform AirDrop with infinite range and zero file size restrictions. By establishing a direct, encrypted peer-to-peer tunnel between sender and receiver, the recipient begins downloading the moment the sender hits send.

### Key Highlights

- **Zero Cloud Intermediary**: Media travels straight from your hard drive to your collaborator's drive without resting on a third-party server.
- **True Resumable Transfers**: Unplugged your drive or shut your laptop lid? Blip resumes the 200GB transfer the moment you reconnect without restarting.
- **Local Network Acceleration**: Automatically detects devices on the same Wi-Fi/LAN and switches to local multi-gigabit speeds.
