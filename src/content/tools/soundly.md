## Overview

Ask Hollywood sound designers and high-speed commercial video editors how they handle sound design, and the answer is almost always **Soundly**.

### Why It Accelerates Sound Design

- **Spot to Timeline**: Select a specific portion of a waveform in Soundly, press a hotkey or drag, and it drops onto your Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or Final Cut Pro timeline at the exact playhead position.
- **Real-Time Pitch & Speed Controls**: Audition any sound effect at double speed, half speed, or reverse with instantaneous pitch alteration before dropping it in.
- **Massive Cloud Library**: Access thousands of pristine 96kHz / 24-bit sound effects stored in the cloud alongside your local sound libraries.
