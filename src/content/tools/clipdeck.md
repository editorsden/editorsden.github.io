## Overview

**Clipdeck** is built for video editors and content teams responsible for repurposing long-form webinars, podcasts, and interviews into high-retention vertical short-form content.

### Highlights

- **Automated Highlight Detection**: AI listens to speaker cadences and keyword spikes to locate the most viral segments.
- **Dynamic Captions & Emojis**: Automatically animates karaoke-style captions timed down to the syllable.
- **Auto-Face Reframing**: Keeps active speakers centered in 9:16 vertical crops automatically.
