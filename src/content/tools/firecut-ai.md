## Overview

FireCut runs directly inside Adobe Premiere Pro as a panel. Unlike cloud auto-editors that force you to download rendered MP4 files, FireCut performs edits natively onto your Premiere timeline tracks with live razor cuts and transitions.

## Key Capabilities

- **Intelligent Silence Cutting**: Analyzes audio thresholds and cuts silence while keeping smooth audio crossfades and customizable padding handles.
- **Automated Multi-Track Multi-Cam Editing**: Distinguishes who is speaking across multiple audio mics and auto-switches angles without losing sync.
- **Dynamic Word-by-Word Captions**: Generates modern animated social captions directly in Premiere's essential graphics system.
- **B-Roll & Zoom Cut Insertion**: Detects key sentence cadences and adds micro-zooms to maintain viewer retention.
