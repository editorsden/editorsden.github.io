## Overview

Ask any experienced Windows video editor for their secret weapon, and **Listary** is almost always at the top of the list. It integrates directly into standard Windows Explorer and File Open/Save dialogues.

### The "Quick Switch" Game-Changer

When you have a folder open in Windows Explorer and click **Import Media** in Premiere Pro or Resolve, Listary automatically detects your open folder and jumps the file dialog directly to that exact path—eliminating the need to re-browse your drive.

### Highlights

- **Instant Open-Dialog Search**: Start typing anywhere in an open dialog to filter thousands of footage files in real-time.
- **Project Folder Bookmarks**: Pin active client drives and jump between camera cards with two keystrokes.
