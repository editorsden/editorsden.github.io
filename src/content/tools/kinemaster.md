## Overview

**KineMaster** brings traditional landscape desktop timeline mechanics directly to smartphones and tablets. It was among the first mobile applications to support true multi-layer video compositing, chroma key green screen extraction, and advanced audio envelope curves on Android and iOS.

## Highlights

- **Multi-Layer Video Support**: Stack multiple video, image, text, and handwriting layers on top of the primary timeline track with blending modes (Screen, Multiply, Overlay).
- **High-Accuracy Chroma Key**: Cleanly extract green or blue backgrounds with real-time spill suppression and edge feathering controls.
- **Audio Envelope Curves**: Fine-tune audio levels frame by frame with keyframe volume points for seamless dialogue-over-music ducking.
- **Asset Store**: Thousands of downloadable royalty-free background music tracks, sound effects, visual transitions, and LUT color filters.
