## Overview

**Cockpit** (by Sequence) transforms the cluttered timeline into an automated command deck. Built specifically for commercial editors and YouTube creators who produce high volumes of episodic content, it eliminates manual file-shuffling.

### Key Capabilities

- **One-Click Project Scaffolding**: Instantly generate production-standard folder structures (01 Footage, 02 Audio, 03 VFX, 04 Exports) with auto-linked templates.
- **Smart Asset Injection**: Keep your team's sound effects, lower thirds, and overlays instantly accessible in a docked panel.
- **Timeline Shortcuts**: Execute multi-step operations like ripple deletes, markers, and audio gain leveling without reaching for sub-menus.
