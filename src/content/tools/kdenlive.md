## Overview

**Kdenlive** (KDE Non-Linear Video Editor) is one of the most mature and capable free, open-source video editing applications available today. Built on Qt and the MLT multimedia framework, Kdenlive offers indie creators and Linux users a professional, non-destructive editing workflow with zero licensing restrictions.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Track Editing**: Arrange unlimited video and audio tracks, lock tracks, and group clips with customizable track compositing.
- **Universal Codec Decoding**: Since it uses FFmpeg under the hood, Kdenlive can directly edit nearly any video, audio, or image container without transcoding beforehand.
- **Automatic Proxy Editing**: Automatically creates lightweight proxy copies of heavy 4K/8K footage for silky-smooth timeline scrubbing, seamlessly switching to full resolution during final render.
- **Integrated Titler & Speech-to-Text**: Built-in titler for 2D graphics and integrated AI speech-to-text model for automatic subtitle track generation.
