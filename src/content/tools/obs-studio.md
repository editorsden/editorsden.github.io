## Overview

**OBS Studio** is the gold standard software for video editors creating editing tutorials, software walkthroughs, gaming captures, and client presentation recordings.

### Essential Features for Editors

- **Isolated Multi-Track Audio**: Record your microphone on Track 1, desktop system audio on Track 2, and video game/call audio on Track 3, giving you full mixing control in post-production.
- **Hardware-Accelerated CFR**: Enforces Constant Frame Rate recording using NVENC, AMD AMF, or Apple VideoToolbox, avoiding out-of-sync audio in Premiere and DaVinci Resolve.
- **Custom Canvas & Remuxing**: Record at native 4K, ultrawide, or 9:16 vertical resolutions, with built-in automatic MKV-to-MP4 remuxing.
