## Overview

**Alight Motion** by Alight Creative is often referred to as "After Effects for your phone." It is the first comprehensive professional motion design and visual effects suite created from the ground up for mobile hardware.

## Key Features

- **Keyframe Velocity Curves**: Unrestricted cubic Bézier easing graph editor for liquid-smooth acceleration and deceleration of all animatable properties.
- **Vector Graphics & Paths**: Draw and animate custom vector shapes directly on canvas, with path trim animations, stroke dashes, and gradient fills.
- **Visual Effects Library**: Over 100 customizable effects including directional motion blur, chromatic aberration, glow, pinch/bulge, and displacement maps.
- **Group & Masking Hierarchy**: Build nested comp hierarchies with alpha mattes, luminance masks, and clipping containers.
- **XML Presets & Sharing**: Export individual effect chains and graphic elements as XML presets that can be shared with other editors.
