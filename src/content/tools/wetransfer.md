## Overview

**WeTransfer** is the universally recognized medium of exchange between editors, production houses, and commercial clients. 

### Why Editors Use It

- **Client Confidence**: Recognized by corporate clients, ad agencies, and studios worldwide.
- **Download Receipts**: Automatically notifies you the moment your client downloads the cut, preventing missed deadlines.
- **Portals & Password Protection**: Pro plans allow custom brand wallpaper and password-protected transfer links.
