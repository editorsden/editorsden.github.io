## Overview

Another masterpiece from **Knights of the Editing Table**, Grave Robber solves Premiere's long-standing nested sequence headache. Instead of double-clicking nests and wrestling with cross-sequence copy-pasting, one key restores all original media clips into the main edit.

## Key Capabilities

- **Attribute Preservation**: Carries over keyframes, opacity, volume levels, Lumetri color grades, and audio track transitions.
- **Batch Un-nesting**: Select multiple nested sequences across a complex assembly and un-nest them simultaneously.
- **Deep Nest Traversal**: Recursively un-nests sequences that were nested inside other nested sequences.
