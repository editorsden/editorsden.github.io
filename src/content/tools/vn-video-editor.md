## Overview

**VN Video Editor** (VlogNow) is widely considered by indie filmmakers and mobile videographers to be the best zero-watermark video editing app on the market. Available natively on iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows, it delivers desktop-grade multi-layer timeline control without slapping watermarks on your finished cuts.

## Why Editors Love VN

- **Zero Watermark on Free Exports**: Unlike competitors that lock clean exports behind steep paywalls, VN allows full 4K 60fps exports at custom bitrates without branding.
- **Precision Speed Curves**: Offers intuitive Bézier curve speed ramping templates (Flash In, Bullet Time, Montage) and completely custom speed points.
- **Full Keyframing**: Smooth keyframe animation for scale, position, rotation, and opacity across video overlays, text graphics, and PIP windows.
- **Cross-Device Project Sharing**: Export `.vnproject` draft archives via AirDrop or cloud drives to seamlessly transfer edits from your smartphone directly to your desktop.
- **Audio Beat Markers**: Automatically detects musical beats on imported audio tracks to simplify cutting on beat.
