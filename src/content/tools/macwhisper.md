## Overview

MacWhisper by Jordi Bruin provides a blazingly fast, privacy-respecting workflow for video editors dealing with long interviews, documentaries, and multicam dialog. It leverages Apple Silicon's CoreML and GPU acceleration to transcribe audio up to 20x faster than real-time.

## Key Capabilities

- **100% Offline & Private**: Zero audio data leaves your workstation. Perfect for NDA-bound client commercials, feature films, and corporate interviews.
- **Export to Any NLE**: One-click export into `.srt`, `.vtt`, `.csv`, `.edl`, or Final Cut Pro `.fcpxml` with markers.
- **Speaker Diarization**: Detects and colors distinct speakers automatically.
- **Multi-language Translation**: Translate foreign language interviews directly into English subtitles on the fly.
