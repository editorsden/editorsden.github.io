## Overview

**LumaFusion** by LumaTouch was the pioneer that proved professional, broadcast-caliber video editing could thrive on a tablet. Winner of Apple's iPad App of the Year, LumaFusion gives mobile journalists, documentary crews, and expedition videographers the precision of a desktop NLE optimized entirely for multi-touch gestures and Apple Pencil.

## Core Pro Capabilities

- **6 4K Video/Audio Tracks**: Support for up to 6 4K/60fps video tracks with titles and graphics, plus 6 additional audio tracks with real-time audio ducking and graphic EQ.
- **External USB-C Drive Editing**: Edit ProRes and 4K footage directly from an attached portable SSD (like Samsung T7/SanDisk Extreme) on USB-C iPads without copying gigabytes of files to internal storage.
- **Color Grading & Custom LUTs**: Extensive color correction controls including 3-point color wheels, curves, and direct `.cube` or `.3dl` LUT import.
- **FCPXML Project Export**: Transition field rough cuts into Apple Final Cut Pro on a Mac desktop with all cuts, audio levels, and titles preserved.
