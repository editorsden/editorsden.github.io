## Overview

Developed by **Knights of the Editing Table**, Excalibur is the single most celebrated productivity extension for Premiere Pro editors. It brings code-editor-style command palettes to the timeline.

## Key Capabilities

- **Instant Effect Application**: Press your shortcut (e.g. `Space + X`), type `warp`, and hit Enter to apply Warp Stabilizer immediately to the selected clip.
- **Custom Multi-Action User Commands**: Chain multiple actions together: add an Adjustment Layer, apply Lumetri Look, set opacity to 80%, and ripple delete empty tracks.
- **Preset Favoriting & Search**: Instantly find any third-party MOGRT or audio preset without touching your mouse.
