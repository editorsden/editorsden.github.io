## Overview

A long-time staple of Mac power users, **Alfred** lets editors navigate massive project file trees and execute custom file actions without taking their hands off the keyboard.

### Key Capabilities

- **File Action Buffer**: Select multiple sound effects or video b-roll clips from search results, add them to Alfred's action buffer, and batch move or open them in your editor.
- **Custom Workflows**: Chain complex tasks like transcoding selected videos with HandBrake or unzipping multi-part client deliveries with a custom keyword.
- **Snippet Expansion**: Quickly paste standardized delivery metadata, YouTube video descriptions, and client invoice notes.
