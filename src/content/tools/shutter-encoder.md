## Overview

Created by professional video editor and colorist Paul Pacifico, **Shutter Encoder** is universally regarded by post-production supervisors as the single most reliable desktop media converter available. 

Unlike standard video converters that always force a slow re-encode, Shutter Encoder features a dedicated **Re-wrap / Without Re-encoding** engine that extracts pristine camera video bitstreams into clean `.mov` or `.mp4` containers in seconds.

### Key Capabilities

- **Edit-Ready Codec Mastering**: Generate clean Apple ProRes 422/4444, Avid DNxHD/DNxHR, GoPro CineForm, or XAVC files.
- **VFR to CFR Normalization**: Eliminates audio-drift sync bugs caused by smartphone or OBS variable frame-rate recordings.
- **Audio & Timecode Injection**: Replace, extract, or sync multi-channel audio tracks and generate broadcast-compliant SMPTE timecode.
- **Burn-In Generation**: Overlay clip names, timecodes, custom watermarks, or subtitle SRT tracks for client review copies.
