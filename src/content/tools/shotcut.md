## Overview

**Shotcut** is a free, open-source, cross-platform video editor developed by Meltytech. Renowned for its lightweight resource footprint and native timeline editing philosophy, Shotcut lets users drag and drop media files directly onto a sequence without project importing or transcoding delays.

## Highlights

- **Native Format Editing**: No import required; native editing across hundreds of audio and video formats and codecs thanks to FFmpeg.
- **Hardware Acceleration**: GPU acceleration and multi-core parallel processing for smooth playback and rapid exports.
- **Comprehensive Video Filters**: Color grading wheels, 3D LUTs, chroma key, text overlays, and audio filters (compressor, limiter, reverb).
- **Portable Version Available**: Can be run from a USB drive without installation on Windows and Linux machines.
