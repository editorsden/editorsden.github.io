## Overview

Developed by **Knights of the Editing Table** (the creators of Excalibur), **Dagger** brings the beloved quick-search experience of After Effects' *FX Console* directly into Adobe Premiere Pro—completely free of charge.

### How It Accelerates Your Timeline

- **Spotlight for Premiere Effects**: Press your custom shortcut, type `blur`, hit enter, and Gaussian Blur is instantly applied to all selected timeline clips.
- **Favorites & Blacklist**: Pin your most frequently used Lumetri, crop, and audio plugins at the top of the search stack, or hide unwanted default effects.
- **Stream Deck & Controller Integration**: Pair Dagger with hardware macro pads or Spell Book to trigger complex effect chains with physical keys.
