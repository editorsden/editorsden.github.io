## Overview

**Apple Final Cut Pro** combines revolutionary timeline architecture with deep optimization for Apple Silicon chips (M1 through M4). Built specifically for editors who prioritize speed, fluid story assembly, and effortless ProRes playback, Final Cut Pro delivers unmatched rendering performance on macOS and iPadOS.

## Signature Features

- **The Magnetic Timeline**: Clips dynamically slide into place and snap together without track collisions, eliminating accidental sync loss between video B-roll and dialogue audio.
- **Apple Silicon Metal Optimization**: Play multiple streams of 8K ProRes RAW in real time without proxy generation. Background rendering ensures you never stare at a progress bar while editing.
- **Object Tracker & Cinematic Mode**: Analyze faces and moving objects to automatically pin titles, graphic callouts, and tracking masks; pull rack focus on iPhone Cinematic video after capture.
- **Compound Clips & Auditions**: Group complex visual layers into clean reusable compound containers, and test multiple takes inside a single audition clip slot with one keypress.
- **Final Cut Pro for iPad**: Touch and Apple Pencil-optimized pro editing interface featuring Live Drawing, Jog Wheel scrubbing, and multi-cam wireless Live Camera recording.
