## Overview

**DaVinci Resolve** by Blackmagic Design is the post-production industry's premier all-in-one non-linear video editing, color grading, audio post, and visual effects application. Originally developed as the gold standard for high-end film color timing, Resolve has expanded into a complete digital film studio used on Oscar-winning feature films, Netflix originals, television broadcasts, and top YouTube productions.

## Why Editors Choose DaVinci Resolve

- **Zero-Roundtrip Pipeline**: Seamlessly toggle between Edit, Cut, Color, Fusion VFX, Fairlight Audio, and Deliver pages without exporting intermediate files or conforming XML/EDL tracks.
- **Node-Based Color Grading**: Unrivaled primary wheels, custom curves, 3D qualifiers, power windows, HDR grading palette, and color management (ACES & DaVinci YRGB Color Managed).
- **DaVinci Neural Engine AI**: State-of-the-art AI features including Magic Mask, Speed Warp optical flow, Voice Isolation, Smart Reframe, and automatic dialogue transcription.
- **True Cross-Platform**: Native Apple Silicon Metal acceleration, multi-GPU Windows workstations, Linux enterprise studio support, and a desktop-class iPadOS app.
- **Generous Free Tier**: The free version has no watermarks, full 4K UHD export support, and comprehensive color and editing capabilities.

## Technical Specifications

- **Supported Formats**: Blackmagic RAW (BRAW), Apple ProRes, Avid DNxHR, RED R3D, ARRI RAW, Sony XAVC, Canon Cinema RAW, H.264, HEVC, and AV1.
- **Multi-User Collaboration**: Built-in Blackmagic Cloud and local PostgreSQL multi-user collaboration with timeline locking, shared chat, and bin permissions.
- **Hardware Integration**: Dedicated Resolve Speed Editor, Micro/Mini Color Panels, and Fairlight Console integration.
