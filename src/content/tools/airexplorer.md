## Overview

For editors who regularly receive 50GB–500GB daily batches from remote film shoots, downloading through browser tabs is a recipe for broken files. **AirExplorer** manages all your cloud accounts in a single, rock-solid desktop environment.

### Why Editors Rely On It

- **Direct Cloud-to-Local Sync**: Transfer files directly from client Google Drive or Dropbox folders to your local video storage with automatic retry upon network drops.
- **Dual-Pane Transfer**: Easily move files between two different cloud providers (e.g. Google Drive directly to OneDrive) without downloading to your local machine first.
- **Bypass Browser Limits**: No more browser memory leaks or failed 40GB zip downloads.
