## Course Overview
Envato Tuts+ provides one of the cleanest, most thorough free entry points into Adobe After Effects. In this multi-hour structured course, complete newcomers are guided through the daunting interface and taught the fundamentals of compositing.

### Curriculum Highlights
1. **Interface & Composition Settings**: Frame rates, pixel aspect ratios, and workspace customization.
2. **Transform Properties**: Anchor points, scale, position, rotation, and opacity mechanics.
3. **Masking & Mattes**: Pen tool fundamentals, track mattes (Alpha vs. Luma), and feathering.
4. **Blending Modes & Adjustment Layers**: Layer stack psychology and non-destructive styling.
5. **Basic Rendering**: Adobe Media Encoder queues and alpha-channel exports.

### Who It's For
Video editors who know Premiere or Resolve and want a structured, distraction-free roadmap into After Effects.
