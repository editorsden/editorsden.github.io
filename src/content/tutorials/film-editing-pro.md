## Why Learn from Film Editing Pro
Film Editing Pro approaches sound not as a technical utility, but as the primary emotional driver of storytelling. Their tutorials demonstrate how Hollywood editors cut trailer sound beds, layer Foley, and build visceral tension using audio risers, braams, and hit points.

### Core Specializations
- **Trailer Sound Design**: Stacking sub-booms, whooshes, and downers to guide audience emotion.
- **Foley & Diegetic Sound**: Breathing life into quiet scenes by building multi-track atmospheric beds.
- **Music Editing & Chopping**: Editing musical scores to hit visual narrative beats without sounding spliced.
