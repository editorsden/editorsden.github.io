## Why Learn from Darren Mostyn
Darren Mostyn is a veteran senior colorist with over 25 years of broadcast credits for BBC, Netflix, Amazon Prime, and ITV. His tutorials are grounded in real deadline pressures, broadcast legal limits, and practical shot-matching workflows.

### Core Specializations
- **Shot Matching Across Cameras**: Rapidly matching Sony FX6, Canon C70, and ARRI Alexa footage across complex scenes.
- **Reading Scopes Accurately**: Navigating waveform monitors, parade scopes, and vectorscopes for perfect skin tones.
- **Broadcast Delivery Standards**: Ensuring your master delivers legal black and white levels without clipping.
