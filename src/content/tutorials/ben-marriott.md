## Why Learn from Ben Marriott
Ben Marriott has established himself as the premier voice in modern 2D animation and motion design. His tutorials focus not just on *how* to click buttons in After Effects, but on the classical Disney 12 principles of animation—squash and stretch, anticipation, staging, and follow-through—applied to vector art and keyframes.

### Core Specializations
- **The Graph Editor Masterclass**: Perfecting speed curves and value graphs for smooth, organic motion.
- **Texture & Organic Movement**: Adding grain, hand-drawn boiling effects, and chromatic distortion in AE.
- **Character & Rigging Basics**: Animating walk cycles, fluid limbs, and morphing vector shapes.
