## Why Learn from FlippedNormals
Henning Sanden and Morten Jaeger are veteran film VFX artists who worked on blockbuster features like *Pacific Rim*, *Batman v Superman*, and *Guardians of the Galaxy*. Their channel brings real studio-level modeling, sculpting, and texturing discipline to everyday artists.

### Core Specializations
- **3D Sculpting Fundamentals**: Anatomy, form, silhouette, and gesture in ZBrush and Blender.
- **Topology & Edge Flow**: Clean mesh construction that deforms correctly for character animation.
- **Baking & Texturing**: Normal maps, roughness channels, and PBR texturing in Substance Painter.
