## Why Learn from Blender Guru
Andrew Price is single-handedly responsible for onboarding more people into the 3D graphics industry than perhaps anyone else on the planet. His flagship "Donut Tutorial" series is a cultural rite of passage that guides absolute beginners from opening Blender to a fully shaded, physically based render.

### Core Specializations
- **The Complete Beginner Pipeline**: Modeling geometry, sculpting, UV unwrapping, and material shading.
- **Photorealistic Lighting**: Understanding inverse square laws, color temperature, and realistic camera exposure.
- **Geometry Nodes & Particles**: Distributing sprinkles, foliage, and environmental debris procedurally.
