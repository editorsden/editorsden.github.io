## Why Learn from Potato Jet
Gene Nagata combines sharp comedic charm with deep technical camera expertise. His real-world comparisons between cinema camera giants (ARRI, RED, Sony Venice) and budget mirrorless rigs give creators honest clarity on where gear matters and where it doesn't.

### Core Specializations
- **Cinema Rigging**: Building ergonomic shoulder rigs, monitor mounts, and follow-focus systems.
- **Real-World Camera Comparisons**: Dynamic range, color rendition, rolling shutter, and low-light shootouts.
- **Lighting Gear in Action**: Testing practical LED tubes, softboxes, and spotlights on creative sets.
