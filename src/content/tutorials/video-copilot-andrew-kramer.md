## Why Video Copilot is Legendary
Andrew Kramer is universally recognized as the grandfather of modern After Effects and VFX education. His historic tutorials—dating from the mid-2000s onwards—trained an entire generation of Hollywood VFX artists, motion designers, and title sequence directors (including his own work on J.J. Abrams' *Star Trek* and *Star Wars: The Force Awakens*).

### Core Specializations
- **Foundational Compositing**: Screen replacements, rotoscoping, track matte logic, and lighting integration.
- **Practical Action FX**: Bullet hits, muzzle flashes, explosions, smoke simulations, and crater impacts.
- **3D Elements Inside AE**: Lighting particles, optical flares, camera tracking, and deep space nebulas.
