## Why Learn from Evan Abrams
Evan Abrams teaches you how to make After Effects do the heavy lifting for you through the power of expressions, shape layer modifiers, and mathematical animation. If you want animations that react dynamically to text changes or sliders, Evan is the teacher to follow.

### Core Specializations
- **After Effects Expressions**: Mastering `wiggle()`, `linear()`, `valueAtTime()`, and slider controls.
- **Shape Layer Modifiers**: Repeater tricks, trim paths, twist, and zig-zag combinations.
- **Procedural Infographics**: Building auto-resizing text boxes and reactive charts for explainers.
