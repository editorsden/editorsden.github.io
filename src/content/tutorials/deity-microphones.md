## Why Learn from Deity Microphones
Clean post-production sound starts on set. The Deity Microphones YouTube channel provides invaluable masterclasses on physical microphone placement, field recorders, timecode syncing, and wireless transmission.

### Core Specializations
- **Hiding Lavalier Microphones**: Taping mics under collars, ties, and chest pieces without fabric rustle.
- **Boom Pole Technique**: Finding the acoustic sweet spot, managing polar patterns, and avoiding boom shadows.
- **Timecode & Dual-System Audio**: Syncing field recorders with cinema cameras using Tentacle and Deity boxes.
