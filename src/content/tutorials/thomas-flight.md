## Why Learn from Thomas Flight
Thomas Flight provides thoughtful, articulate, and timely video essays on current cinema and television (*Oppenheimer*, *Succession*, *Everything Everywhere All At Once*). He focuses deeply on the editing choices—sound bridges, montage theory, temporal manipulation—that distinguish today's greatest filmmakers.

### Core Specializations
- **Montage & Nonlinear Editing**: How modern films compress years into minutes without losing narrative clarity.
- **Soundscape Analysis**: How audio silence, room tones, and sudden cuts enhance psychological tension.
- **Contemporary Visual Grammar**: Analyzing modern techniques like chaotic handheld documentary styles versus locked-off formalism.
