## Why Learn from Colin and Samir
While not a traditional software tutorial channel, Colin and Samir are required viewing for anyone who edits for YouTube and digital platforms. They interview the world's top creators and editors (MrBeast, Mark Rober, MKBHD) and analyze the storytelling mechanics that drive billions of views.

### Core Specializations
- **The First 30 Seconds**: Hook construction, visual payoffs, and avoiding narrative drag.
- **Story Arcs in Digital Video**: Three-act structure adapted for algorithmic platforms and short attention spans.
- **The Editor-Creator Dynamic**: How lead editors collaborate with talent to shape cultural hits.
