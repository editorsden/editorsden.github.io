## Why Learn from Cullen Kelly
Cullen Kelly is an active Hollywood commercial and feature film colorist whose teaching focuses on modern, scene-referred color science (DaVinci Wide Gamut, ACES). He teaches editors how to abandon destructive display-referred grading and build bulletproof, repeatable node architectures.

### Core Specializations
- **Scene-Referred Color Science**: Grading in DaVinci Wide Gamut / Intermediate for maximum dynamic range and smooth roll-off.
- **Modular Fixed Node Trees**: Structuring your project so exposure, balance, contrast, and creative looks never conflict.
- **Designing Film Print Emulations**: Creating custom 3D LUTs that emulate Kodak and Fuji film stocks organically.
