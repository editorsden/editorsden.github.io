## Why Learn from School of Motion
School of Motion is the premier training institution for motion designers worldwide. While they offer intensive paid bootcamps, their public YouTube channel is a treasure trove of free tutorials, design theory, and candid interviews with creative directors from Giant Ant, Buck, and Ordinary Folk.

### Core Specializations
- **Animation Principles & Kinetic Typography**: The technical discipline of typography, hierarchy, and easing.
- **Pipeline Workflows**: How Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects, and Cinema 4D connect in agency pipelines.
- **Career Strategy**: How to price motion design work, build a standout showreel, and navigate creative burnout.
