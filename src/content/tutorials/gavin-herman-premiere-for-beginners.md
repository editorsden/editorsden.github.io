## Course Overview
Gavin Herman's definitive beginner masterclass is one of the most widely recommended zero-to-hero video editing courses on YouTube. In just over an hour, it takes someone who has never opened video editing software and walks them through every essential phase of post-production.

### Curriculum Breakdown
1. **Workspace & Ingestion**: Creating projects, organizing bins, and setting up scratch disks.
2. **Timeline Essentials**: Source monitors, inserting clips, ripple trims, and the razor tool.
3. **Audio Fundamentals**: Volume keyframing, adding music tracks, and voice normalization.
4. **Titles & Lower Thirds**: Essential Graphics text styling, font pairing, and safe margins.
5. **Color & Lumetri Basics**: Balancing exposure, white balance, and contrast.
6. **Exporting for YouTube & Socials**: Bitrates, codecs (H.264/HEVC), and optimal presets.

### Who It's For
Absolute beginners looking for a structured, distraction-free foundation before jumping into specialized editing.
