## Why Cooke Optics TV is Invaluable
Produced by legendary British cinema lens maker Cooke, this channel features candid, unfiltered interviews with Academy Award-winning Directors of Photography (*Dune*, *Blade Runner 2049*, *1917*, *Skyfall*).

### Core Specializations
- **The Psychology of Focal Length**: Why a 28mm lens communicates vulnerability while an 85mm isolates a character.
- **Anamorphic vs. Spherical Glass**: Flaring, bokeh shape, barrel distortion, and emotional texture.
- **Lighting for Faces**: How master cinematographers flatter actors across different skin tones and character moods.
