## Why Every Frame a Painting is Mandatory Viewing
Though the channel concluded in 2016, Tony Zhou and Taylor Ramos created the gold standard by which all video essays and film studies are measured. Every single episode is an immaculate masterclass in editorial precision, analyzing why great directors cut where they cut.

### Legendary Episodes
- **The Bad and Better of Jackie Chan**: Visual comedy, impact framing, and avoiding Western cutaways.
- **David Fincher - And the Other Way is Wrong**: The psychology of cinematic eye-trace and disciplined camera tracking.
- **Chuck Jones - The Evolution of an Animator**: Pacing, timing, and pauses in visual storytelling.
- **Buster Keaton - The Art of the Gag**: Geometry, perspective, and continuous shots in physical comedy.
