## Why Learn from Aputure
Lighting is what separates amateur footage from cinematic imagery. Aputure's *4-Minute Film School* series breaks down the exact lighting diagrams, diffusion types, and contrast ratios used in major motion pictures and TV dramas.

### Core Specializations
- **Contrast Ratios & Key-to-Fill**: Setting 4:1 and 8:1 lighting ratios for dramatic cinematic mood.
- **Diffusion & Shadow Softness**: Using negative fill, book lights, and grid diffusion to shape soft facial falloff.
- **Mixed Lighting & Kelvin Mastery**: Blending warm tungstens with cool daylights to establish visual depth.
