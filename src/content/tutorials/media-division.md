## Why Media Division is Unprecedented
Media Division does not produce standard YouTube videos; they release full-length, broadcast-quality technical documentaries on cinematography. Their famous Stanley Kubrick f/0.7 lens recreation and comprehensive guides to vintage glass are legendary within the motion picture industry.

### Core Specializations
- **Optical Science & Mechanics**: Chromatic aberration, lens coatings, flange distances, and optical element geometry.
- **Adapting Vintage Cinema Lenses**: Modifying Canon FD, Contax Zeiss, and Leica R lenses for modern full-frame sensors.
- **Extreme Low-Light Cinematography**: Pushing sensors to their physical limits with fast aperture glass and moonlight exposure.
