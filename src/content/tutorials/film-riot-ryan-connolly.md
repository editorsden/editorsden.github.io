## Why Film Riot is Essential
Ryan Connolly launched Film Riot over 15 years ago, democratizing cinema production for millions of young creators worldwide. Through behind-the-scenes breakdowns of his own award-winning short films (*PROXIMITY*, *BALLOON*), Ryan proves that high-concept storytelling requires ingenuity, not multimillion-dollar budgets.

### Core Specializations
- **Practical On-Set Effects**: Blood splatters, camera shakes, gun recoil, and miniature work on zero budget.
- **Indie Set Lighting**: Lighting moody interiors, night shoots, and dynamic silhouettes with basic lights.
- **Director-Editor Collaboration**: Directing scenes with the edit in mind to prevent missing coverage.
