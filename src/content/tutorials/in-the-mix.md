## Why Learn from In The Mix
While primarily music-focused, Michael from In The Mix has produced the single clearest visual explanations of compression, equalization, and harmonic saturation on the internet. His visual demonstrations make complex acoustic engineering immediately understandable for video editors.

### Core Specializations
- **Compression Demystified**: Threshold, ratio, attack, and release explained with dynamic visual meters.
- **Carving Frequencies with EQ**: Eliminating muddiness in the 200–500Hz range and opening high-end air.
- **Mastering & Loudness Standards**: Understanding LUFS, true peak limiters, and platform loudness targets.
