## Why Learn from The Average Editor
The Average Editor provides transparent, real-world advice focused on the business and craft of commercial video editing. Rather than flashy party tricks, this channel teaches clean timeline hygiene, client revision management, and subtle pacing adjustments that elevate average cuts to professional standards.

### Core Specializations
- **Timeline Hygiene & Organization**: Project structures that prevent crashes and make assistant handoffs seamless.
- **Pacing Refinement**: Detecting narrative fat and eliminating awkward pauses in documentary and commercial edits.
- **Freelance Career Advice**: Pricing, contracts, and communicating effectively with creative directors.
