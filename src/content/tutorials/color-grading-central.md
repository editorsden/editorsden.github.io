## Why Learn from Color Grading Central
Denver Riddle and Color Grading Central helped popularize digital color grading for indie creators. Their tutorials focus on understanding the color wheel, complementary palettes, and using LUTs as creative starting points rather than crutches.

### Core Specializations
- **Color Theory Fundamentals**: Orange & teal separation, triadic harmonies, and emotional color psychology.
- **Curves & Primary Wheels**: Lift, Gamma, Gain mechanics and hue-versus-saturation adjustments.
- **Skin Tone Protection**: Isolating and preserving natural skin tones while pushing stylized grades in the background.
